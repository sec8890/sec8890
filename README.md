hi, I'm Sophia 👋

I work across art, technology, and material research — building tactile instruments, DIY biodegradable paints, and small web projects for other people's writing.

🧪 Experimental Paint Archive — an open-source collection of biodegradable paint recipes, designed to be safe and easy to make at home
🎛️ Squishy Music — a tactile musical instrument, still in progress
🔌 Copper-sensor sound pieces shown at the New York Transit Museum, Yale CCAM Fluxus, and Littlefield in Brooklyn
📖 Pulp Press — a literary magazine where I design a custom site for each author's short fiction

Most of my projects come back to the same question: how systems — payment infrastructure, materials, instruments — can be redesigned to work for more people.
